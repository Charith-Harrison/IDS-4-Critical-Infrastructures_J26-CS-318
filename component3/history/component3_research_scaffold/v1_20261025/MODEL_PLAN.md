# Future model and evaluation plan - nothing fitted

This follows the already frozen research comparison. No learner instance, model parameters,
training matrix, score or final test selection is produced by this scaffold.

| Method | What it can see | What it asks |
|---|---|---|
| A: protocol checks | Present protocol syntax and legitimate association checks at their availability time | Is shape/length/function/echo invalid? Valid injections may pass |
| B: current-message RF | Current ADU/public map only | What can the planned learner do without history? |
| C: simple causal rules | Same legitimate public policy/map and eligible earlier evidence as D | Does ON contradict observed mode 0; does readback contradict latest acknowledged setpoint? |
| D: contextual RF | Same learner family as B plus declared causal history | Does learned context add anything beyond B or the rules? |
| B-wire / D-wire | Respective inputs plus current numeric TID/endpoints/ports for both | Does an apparent gain survive equal access to wire nuisance cues? |

RF is the proposal's planned Random Forest family, not a newly tuned recommendation.
Do not give B request-derived fields disguised as current FC3 response data. Both semantic
methods receive equivalent public configuration. M2 testing means a supplied alternate map,
not discovering meaning from unknown addresses. Do not use private scenario flags to map roles.

The lab policy is deliberately simple and fully expressible by C. D may match C, add no
benefit, or perform worse. That is a valid result. No scenario/noise/feature is added just to
make an ML method win. Unknown anchors or untrusted writers require abstention, not invented
labels. Protocol validity and parser success are separate from permission and ground truth.

## Future sequence

1. Verify full collection and integrity; record academic objective and remaining limitations.
2. Have the student implement the separate preprocessing plan, with synthetic unit checks
   before accessing actual training observations. Freeze input/decision contracts.
3. Allocate accepted whole related groups according to the existing roster. No new 70/15/15
   split. Keep qualification excluded and final stress tests separate.
4. Fit B and D only on training groups; use the same learner family and comparable tuning
   budgets. Rules and task definitions come from the public frozen policy, not test labels.
   Any learned transformations or decision thresholds follow training/validation discipline.
5. Freeze code, input definitions, parameters, thresholds, abstention and metric denominators.
   Then run final evaluation once under the declared protocol. Do not keep adjusting to final
   results. Later exploratory analyses must be labelled exploratory.

The frozen budget is 32 train runs, 16 validation, 24 final-repeat and four separate eight-run
stress tests. Replication counts are coverage choices, not a formal power guarantee. Runs are
fresh executions of one deterministic software process, with related copies/twins contained.
Repeated days, seeds, blocks or payload rows do not establish independent attack campaigns.

## What future reporting must show

- Per-task/class/run/condition support, genuine normal controls and actual prevalence.
- Confusion counts and per-class precision/recall/F1, plus macro summaries on the same cohort.
  Overall accuracy alone can conceal missed rare attacks. Explain undefined metrics if support
  is absent rather than silently treating them as evidence of performance.
- Normal-only and mixed-normal false alarms with denominators; all-traffic coverage, warm-up,
  abstention and causal decision latency. Compare common supported examples transparently.
- Paired comparisons at whole-run/related-group level and uncertainty with limited cell counts
  disclosed. Never treat thousands of correlated ADUs as thousands of independent trials.
- Primary repeat results separately from held-out values, supplied map, ordering and timing.
  Also report B-wire/D-wire; show any dependence on numeric TID, headers, values or schedule.

Audit scheduling/template position, endpoint/unit/address, timing, repeated payloads, missingness,
static patterns and source metadata for shortcuts. Main roster order is not counterbalanced
across roles: host/time drift may be confounded with roles. Qualification established engineered
controls and lineage, not an absence of all shortcuts. No shortcut classifier is fitted here.

If full evidence lacks support, comparable controls or causal observability, narrow the claim
or retain UNSUPPORTED. Do not fix the scientific question through oversampling or a favourable
split. Even an excellent future score would apply to these two declared interventions in this
testbed; it would not establish plant impact, all Modbus attacks, real MITM capability, or unseen
industrial campaigns. The old application can later consume a locked detector through its
event interface; its old binary accuracy is not a result of this new study.
