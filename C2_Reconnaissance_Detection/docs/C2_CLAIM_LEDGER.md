## 1. Purpose and Claim-Discipline Taxonomy

To prevent conflating proposal aspirations, literature assertions, methodological choices, and empirical measurements, every major claim in the C2 research component is classified into one of six strict epistemic categories:

1. **`Prior literature`**: Findings or background reported by published external studies (documented in `docs/C2_LITERATURE_MATRIX.md`).
2. **`Approved proposal / TAF`**: Objectives, hypotheses, or architectural targets defined in `IT4010-TAF-2026_J26-CS-318.pdf` or `IT4010_Research-Proposal_J26-CS-318_IT23269002.pdf`.
3. **`Implemented method`**: Verified properties of our preprocessing, sequence reconstruction, feature engineering, splitting, or model training code (`src/*.py`, `configs/default.yaml`).
4. **`Observed result`**: Exact empirical measurements computed from the supplied datasets or out-of-fold model evaluations (`artifacts/*.json`, `artifacts/*.csv`).
5. **`Interpretation`**: Reasoned analytical explanation connecting observed results to underlying protocol mechanics or model inductive biases.
6. **`Limitation`**: Demonstrated boundaries of the dataset, labelling pipeline, experimental scope, or generalizability (`docs/C2_LIMITATIONS.md`).
