# Draft methodology amendment — for supervisor review

**Status: NOT_APPROVED / acceptance NOT_RECORDED.** Prepared25October2026. This document does not replace the signed original proposal or claim that its approval extends to this change.

## Original plan and reason for change

The individual proposal pp.21–24 makes CICModbusDataset2023 primary, MSU New Gas Pipeline secondary and a defensive testbed optional. Its planned context includes windows, transitions, timing, permission/role and value features; pp.27–29 also proposes grouped CV, feature-group ablations and external validation.

The conservative CIC evidence study could not support independent-campaign three-class supervised evaluation: class-bearing campaigns and partition support were insufficient, and accepted attack evidence was dominated by malformed or repeated patterns. A diagnostic timing-window change is not a justified new label policy. CIC sources and audit results remain preserved; no external targets/values/times were imported as truth.

MSU, water-storage and all other external datasets remain excluded under the student's current scope. The TAF p.6 permits primary simulated testbed data, but that does not by itself approve changing the individual proposal's primary dataset or its research claims.

## Proposed revised research question

**In a controlled single-writer Modbus/TCP software testbed with supplied public maps and trustworthy earlier observations, how does explicit causal command/response context change detection of two protocol-valid intervention mechanisms relative to current-message Random Forest and simple protocol checks, and how does it compare with fully specified causal rules?**

This is a representation/evidence study. It is not a claim of a novel RF algorithm, learned physical semantics, industrial deployment readiness, broad attack coverage or ML superiority over rules.

## Prospective work if a stronger claim is required

Freeze a new protocol before observing new evaluation results. Develop any altered features/algorithms only with training/validation data. Keep the original final sets available only for explicitly exploratory comparisons. Acquire fresh prospective recordings with independently implemented or operated systems if the objective is transfer beyond this generator; new random seeds alone do not supply that independence.

Specify realistic legitimate behavior, maps, writer/anchor trust, loss and ambiguity handling before selecting attacks. Include genuine changed values and authorized writes so value/context checks do not simply encode target labels by construction. Broader untrusted-anchor/multi-writer regimes may make truth unidentifiable from wire traffic; retain abstention or narrow the question instead of inventing labels.

Predeclare the residual-removal and other defensible representation ablations, matched method information, training-only transformations, task coverage, class/task supports, false alarms, abstentions and process-status denominators. Keep rules and protocol/signature baselines. Measure end-to-end latency/memory/load on documented hardware separately from prepared-row model timing. Use an independently reviewed label process and prospective splits; report negative results.
