## Identifier meanings and independence

The source README device roles provide externally documented direction: normal SCADA `.3`, secure SCADA `.2`, normal IED `.5`, secure IEDs `.4` and `.8`, attacker `.7`, central agent `.6`. This supports endpoint-role interpretation but does not prove every request from a SCADA/attacker is reconnaissance. The same host can generate ordinary or other-attack traffic.

`session_id` includes direction and unit. Reciprocal requests and responses therefore fall in separate identifiers, although they depend on the same transaction and attack episode. The suffix `sXXXX` is an upstream naming coordinate; its creation source is absent. TCP ports, handshakes and sequence evidence must be recovered from PCAP to construct real connection generations. A tuple reused over weeks cannot be presented as a single independent TCP session.

`capture_group` is an implementation mapping, not original acquisition metadata. Source README's 100 MB rotation statement particularly invalidates treating each `substation-N.pcap` or `veth*-N.pcap` as an independent experiment merely from its name. Similarly, distinct dates need not imply independent attack tools/scenarios or independent configuration. Final campaign grouping must use full raw time ranges, logs and overlap/mirror checks. `capture_campaign_summary.csv` and `split_feasibility.json` own the independently recovered counts and feasibility verdict.

## New forensic reconstruction boundary

The review's reconstruction starts again from immutable PCAP/log files and keeps original processed label assertions separate. Each candidate should retain raw path/hash, frame/message locator, bytes/decoded direction, request/response linkage, raw log reference, clock-alignment basis, matching ambiguity and state. Sequence position is assigned only after ordering within a declared dependency unit. It is not copied as source truth. Exact/contextual/response-associated/ambiguous/unmatched/heuristic states are evidence classes, not an instruction to relabel every nearby packet as attack.

Raw-source reconstruction results are reported in the new raw-evidence outputs and the full report; this document does not elevate the old 158-row mapped workbook to original ground truth. No final scaler, rebalance operation, LSTM fit or hyperparameter selection is authorized in this audit.

## Source findings recovered during this review

The final raw candidate table contains 468 real on-wire read-request candidates. Only 148 are `supported_contextual`; 320 retain `ambiguous` attribution and are not final positive labels. Exact clock attribution is not established. Candidates are separate from the 8699 supplied positive label assertions: the goal is to recover initiating behavior even where its processed label was normal or absent, not to maximize agreement with the old class.

The raw recovery matches 8687 of the 8699 supplied positive processed rows to available capture observations; the remaining 12 are in the unexplained date-less attached-flow snippet. Repeated observations remain explicitly recorded. The dominant 8400 FC5/len3 supplied positives have actual wire function byte `0x85` and exception byte `0x03`; the processed FC5 field therefore omits the high exception bit. This is source-byte evidence for response semantics, not proof of which code line historically normalized the field or assigned the label. The paired requests and original non-recon coil-write/brute-force log evidence must remain separate from actual read-range probing.

The independent rotation check shows `substation-1` through `substation-17` share adjacent source-time boundaries separated by only 16 to 19971 microseconds. Thus the old configured substation capture groups split a continuous acquisition. Their separate names and zero row-index overlap cannot substantiate independent campaigns. `substation-0` falls later in source time and has a large gap; source-time ordering, not numeric suffix, controls chronological interpretation. `split_feasibility.json` marks a confirmatory independent three-way split `UNSUPPORTED` and keeps the true independent-campaign count unknown.
